## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need evidence-based prioritization. Juno turns noisy Slack, tickets, and strategy docs into a ranked, cited backlog they can defend.

## Solution overview

**Retrieval strategy:** Modular RAG

It's important to have accuracy, citations and cost predictability. Long context is not preferred due to the large volume of data. Hybrid is not necessary for deep reasoning and would increase cost.

A mid-sized model uses a vector DB to retrieve only the most relevant segments per query.

## Retrieval requirements (RAG)

- **Sources:** (a) company watchlist database, (b) licensed market data GraphQL sbfinancials query, (c) proprietary editorial articles in pubedit database

Quantity: Watchlist database query 1 table with 2 average million records, 1 GraphQL query to retrieve the data, 1 query for news publish through average 1500 records. 
Why: The database queries are necessary to get the current list for customer value. For this reason, the recap is weekly instead of daily or intraday.
- **Chunking / indexing:** Hybrid (Semantic + Keyword)
- **Grounding rule:** The recap cites referenced articles with URLs. Where supporting articles are not found, direct data is returned without additional language. If required data cannot be retrieved the recap is not generated and is flagged.
- **Freshness:** Once per week refresh

## Out of scope

Decisions that cannot be cited to a source in the knowledge base. Anything Juno is not allowed to retrieve or act on without a human.
